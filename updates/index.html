Version 1.6.0

Your dashboard, beside the page

Open the full workspace in Chrome's side panel or a tab.

↗
01

Choose where to work

  • The popup now has separate icon buttons for the side panel and a tab.
  • Choose where popup tasks open in Settings, including when you edit a task.
  • A burger menu makes dashboard sections accessible in narrow windows and the side panel.
  • Press Alt+Shift+A to open or close the side panel. If it is unassigned, set it in your browser shortcut settings.
  • In a narrow side panel, search stays visible and extra filters open when you hover or focus the search block.
Version 1.5.0

Shortcuts and a live clock

Use keyboard shortcuts to manage tasks and see the local date and time in the dashboard.

↗
01

Shortcuts at hand

  • Press N to capture, / to search, or G followed by 1–5 to change sections.
  • With a task open, E edits it, F changes daily focus, P postpones it, and X completes it.
  • Press ? or hover over the keyboard icon beside Settings in the top toolbar to see every shortcut and where to change the browser shortcut.
02

Local date and time

  • The dashboard header shows your local date, weekday, and time in your language, updating every minute.

Recurring tasks and smaller screens

The next repeat stays on schedule, and task controls remain usable on narrow screens.

↗
01

Tasks and editing

Version 1.4.3

A clearer way to manage tasks and projects

The latest changes to the icon counter, task actions, and project cards.

↗
01

A badge count that works for you

  • Show unfinished tasks scheduled for today.
  • Show overdue tasks.
  • Or show the combined total of today's and overdue tasks.
02

Task list and quick actions

  • Postpone a task by 24 hours from the task list, popup, or Overview cards using the clock-and-arrow action.
  • Select “No project” in the project filter to show tasks without a project.
  • The dashboard tab icon and title follow your chosen counter mode.
03

Project cards

  • Rename a project with the pencil beside its name; leaving the field saves it.
  • Use the “+” icon to add a task to the project.
Version 1.4.0

Your planning space, from start to finish

Everything available in this release, organized by what you want to do.

↗
01

Capture and manage tasks

  • Add a task in the popup or the full dashboard without creating an account.
  • Set a priority, estimate, start time, deadline, and project; edit details whenever plans change.
  • Complete tasks, reopen them, and review their history in the task details.
02

Daily focus and task overview

  • Choose the tasks that matter today; unfinished focus tasks stay in view.
  • See today's other tasks and overdue work separately in the popup and dashboard.
  • Track how many tasks you completed today in the overview.
03

Routines, reminders, and rescheduling

  • Repeat tasks daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly.
  • Get a reminder before a task or at a specific date and time you choose.
  • Optionally move overdue work forward with your own rescheduling rule.
04

Projects and strategy

  • Group related tasks into projects and keep each project's work together.
  • Connect projects to strategic goals with outcomes, review dates, and visible progress.
  • Save project task recommendations for a day and add them to your daily focus.
05

A dashboard that fits you

  • Reorder, resize, and hide overview cards to shape your workspace.
  • Switch between light, dark, and system themes, plus flat and morphism designs.
  • Use NITI in English, Spanish, or Russian.
06

Find and organize work

  • Search, filter, and sort tasks by project, status, priority, schedule, and more.
  • Select several tasks for bulk changes and quick actions.
  • Archive finished work or move it to trash, with restore controls when needed.
07

Your data stays yours

  • Core planning works offline, with tasks and projects stored in your browser.
  • Export a backup and restore it when moving to another browser or device.
  • Optionally import selected Zenkit lists once; NITI does not keep syncing them.
08

Small comforts

  • Open the dashboard from the popup or with the Alt+Shift+N shortcut.
  • See update notes after a new version arrives and dismiss the notice until the next update.
  • Copy the extension link from the popup or dashboard sidebar, or leave optional feedback.
  • See your local date and time in the dashboard header, updated every minute.
